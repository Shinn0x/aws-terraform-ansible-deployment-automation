Small print, big answers

The questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Email or call us, any day of the week.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive in 2 to 4 business days, charged and ready to use. Delivery is free on every order.

What if a product isn't right for me?

Send it back within 30 days for a full refund. We'll email you a prepaid return label.

What does the warranty cover?

Every Agex device has a two-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship.

Do the cloud products need a subscription?

No. Nimbus and Keystone work without any monthly fee.

Can I order for my company?

Yes. Our team can set you up with volume pricing, invoicing and help with installing Pulse and Mesh.

How do I order?

Email sales@agex.example or call us with the product you want. We’ll confirm the price, delivery date and payment by email.
