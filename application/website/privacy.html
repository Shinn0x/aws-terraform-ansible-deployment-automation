Section 01Who we are

Agex ("we", "us") designs and sells cloud, hardware and IT products. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website, use our products, contact us or place an order, and how we use it.

Section 02What we collect

We only collect what we need to serve you:

  • Contact details such as your name, email address and phone number when you write to us or order.
  • Delivery details such as your shipping address.
  • Order details such as what you bought and when. Card numbers are handled by our payment provider and never stored by us.
  • Basic technical data such as your browser type and the pages you visit, kept in server logs to keep the site secure.

Section 03Data on your products

Files on Nimbus and keys on Keystone are encrypted on your own device. We can't read them. Devices send us anonymous health data, such as error codes, only if you turn this on.

Section 04How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To send updates about your order and your products, and marketing only if you opted in.
  • To keep our website and products secure and improve them.

Section 05Cookies

This website doesn't use cookies or tracking scripts. Our web server keeps basic logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for a short time to keep the site secure.

Section 06Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We share it only with services that help us run Agex, such as payment providers, delivery companies and email tools, and only as much as they need to do their job.

Section 07How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting. Other information is deleted when we no longer need it.

Section 08Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to how we use it. Email us and we'll help.

Section 09Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and let customers know by email.

Section 10Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Email privacy@agex.example or use our contact page.
