It started with one idea.

Agex began with a simple question: what would technology look like if it just worked, and kept working?

Small team, big ideas

We were tired of gadgets that looked clever in the ad and broke a month later. So we started building the products we wished existed, and we promised to support every one of them ourselves.

Today Agex products are used in more than 40 countries. The team is bigger, but the promise hasn't changed: if something isn't right, we'll fix it.
